How books are judged

The collection separates eligibility, vetoes and quality. A high score cannot override an exclusion or a veto. The Books view includes every eligible, non-vetoed source work, including poor fits.

1. Collect and identify source books

Read the submitted page, its main content, comments and replies. Expand comments where access permits, and record missing or inaccessible material. Only books named or directly linked in that source become candidates. External research can establish identity and evidence; it does not add unrelated titles.

Resolve author, title, aliases, editions and nominated scope. A whole series and one volume are different scopes. Merge duplicate mentions of the same work and retain every distinct numbered source. Repeated mentions are not independent evidence of quality. Ambiguous identities remain in the source audit until resolved.

For an existing work, read its prior assessments and reassess the gates, all criteria, deductions, dates and confidence using old and new evidence. Explain what changed or remained. Update its existing entry; preserve prior analyses as history. A work belongs to at most one of the recommended, vetoed and excluded lists.

Duplicate titles, aliases and previously covered series members are documented; only unique new works receive new gate and score assessments. 2. Apply hard exclusions first

  1. Book form — current rule. /* BOOK_FORM */ Non-book references stay outside all three book ledgers.
  2. Fiction — current rule. /* FICTION */
  3. H3 — Technical training and reference exclusion — current rule. /* REFERENCE */
  4. H4 — Language availability — current rule. /* LANGUAGE */ An English or Croatian edition qualifies, even when the original language differs.

Assess the main purpose and the work required of its reader. Clear prose, historical introductions and conceptual explanations do not admit a technical curriculum, proof collection or specialist reference. Historical surveys and annotated chronologies can qualify when their explanatory prose has value beyond locating references. Difficulty or classroom adoption alone does not exclude a genuinely explanatory history.

Investigate uncertain cases rather than classify from a title. Hard-excluded books receive neither ordinary nor counterfactual scores. The biography exceptions below cannot override these exclusions. Earlier analyses preserve the rules and judgments used at their dates; the latest rule review determines current membership.

3. Check vetoes

After hard exclusions, a veto applies when one of these conditions is present at a serious level:

  1. V1 — Epistemic failure. The central argument depends on material factual errors, distorted sources, invalid causal inference, concealed uncertainty or obsolete information presented as current.
  2. V2 — Ideological capture. Allegiance replaces evidence; counterexamples are ignored, caricatured or treated asymmetrically.
  3. V3 — No transferable intellectual payload. Little mechanism, constraint, model, heuristic or general lesson survives removal of local detail. Mathematical, scientific and high-tech biographies or memoirs have the specified exception.
  4. V4 — Hype or ephemerality dependence — current rule. /* V4 */
  5. V5 — Religious doctrine or ideological extremism — current rule. /* V5 */
  6. V6 — Partisan mobilization — current rule. /* V6 */

For V4, inspect the evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, failure conditions and counterarguments. A forecast or speculative topic alone is insufficient; expert standing alone also does not establish the required safeguards. For V5, identify a central doctrinal purpose or specific advocacy of extremism. A manifesto label, activism, institutional criticism, technological vision or proposed reform does not establish either condition. Discussion of religion or extremism is not itself endorsement.

Apply each veto independently and record the serious defect and supporting evidence. Do not replace a withdrawn V4 or V5 with V1 or V2 merely because a book takes a position. Retain another veto only when its own test is supported.

V6 separately tests dominant recruitment, allegiance-building and campaign attacks, across political positions. A book can pass V5 and still fail V6. A policy conclusion, institutional criticism or reform proposal alone is insufficient. The October 2026 category review applies revised H3 and V6 while preserving existing numerical assessments; two previously unscored works admitted under H3 receive their first baseline assessments.

Record the rule, reason and confidence. A low score or weak topic fit is not another veto. Each vetoed work retains a full counterfactual assessment: suspend only the veto gate, keep the identified flaws and deductions, and calculate its score. That score does not make it recommended. The Vetoed view displays this saved score, unaffected by custom settings.

4. Assess ten positive criteria

The profile favors pragmatic explanation of how science, technology, production, logistics, energy, trade, finance, institutions and strategic power interact. It assumes a reader trained in theoretical physics, computation and numerical simulation. Popular exposition can therefore have strong factual quality but low additional utility for this reader.

Assess a linear attainment x between zero and each criterion's own maximum m. The ten maxima sum to 100. Only evidence, systems integration and information density use a quadratic profile.

Half attainment earns one quarter of the maximum on a quadratic criterion. Causal depth is linear, with maximum 12. Never square a total, deduction or already transformed contribution. When weights change, retain the assessed fraction of the old maximum unless new evidence warrants a changed assessment.

Information density means useful learning

Count the distinct facts, mechanisms, evidence, distinctions and cases learned for the reading required. Several examples of one idea can be highly educational: a shared framework does not make new history or a new boundary condition redundant. Shortness, difficult prose, jargon and topic appeal do not establish density. Deduct for material that adds little understanding, and assess the whole book.

Attainment anchors: 9–10 sustained high learning yield with little removable material; 7–8 substantial learning with some lower-yield passages; 5–6 useful content mixed with substantial repetition or low-yield explanation; 3–4 a small payload spread over much of the book; 0–2 very little educational content for its length. These judgments are not ratios or measured facts per page. The quadratic contribution remains D²/10.

The current focused review covers Why Nations Fail, The Righteous Mind and A Thousand Brains. Their evidence and confidence appear in Details. Other density values remain prior estimates and are marked as not recalibrated. Publisher descriptions alone cannot establish paragraph-level density; a full reading or representative text samples are stronger evidence. The exact rules below contain the assessment protocol.

Apply five separate deductions

  • T11: 0 or −10. Deduct when the content predominantly covers racial history or civil movements, ecology or environmental issues, urban history, political or media celebrities, American-focused issues in both narrative and influence, American-tragedy themes, single-nation history or disease.
  • T12: 0 or −10. Deduct when war, military doctrine, politics or political intelligence, finance, media, power or philosophy is the central topic. These are welcome as applications or supporting domains around technology, energy, mathematics, physics, engineering, computing, logistics, resources, scaling, production, quality, invention and improvement.
  • H13: historical-topic age. For a clearly bounded historical subject, use the end of its central period: −min(12, max(0, review year − period end) / 25). Use fractional years, not completed blocks. An explicitly timeless or broad cross-period account with no single central period receives zero. Publication year is not a substitute for the topic endpoint.
  • P14: original publication age. −min(10, max(0, review year − original year) / 10). Reprints, translations and revised editions do not restart the clock. Record approximate composition dates for ancient works, with confidence. Use the first volume's original year for a nominated series.
  • O15: Amazon rating count. Use one matching product's total ratings: fewer than 10 → −20; 10–99 → −10; 100–999 → −5; at least 1,000 → 0. The bands do not add together.

T11 and T12 are independent and can total −20. A thematic deduction and a veto test different conditions. The saved review year is 2026. Keep full precision for calculation, sorting and thresholds; display two decimals. Rounded displayed components can differ slightly from a displayed total.

5. Record evidence and uncertainty

Use publisher, author, library, primary-text and specialist-review evidence to establish identity, purpose, dates, claims and limitations. Publisher praise does not prove quality. Distinguish facts, interpretation and inference. Assign high, moderate or low confidence where supported; leave unknown facts unknown.

Verify the title and author for Amazon observations. Total ratings are not written reviews. Do not sum editions or marketplaces. Record marketplace, product identifier, URL, retrieval date and snapshot limits. After unsuccessful retrieval attempts, an unavailable count remains unknown and uses the prescribed zero obscurity deduction. This fallback is not a claim that the book has zero ratings. Aggregator descriptions and reader counts require separate verification; the Books Guru capture contains mismatched descriptions.

Record available reader aggregates from Book Marks, LibraryThing, StoryGraph, Goodreads and Amazon with attribution. Reader popularity is separate from the criterion assessment. Each source analysis contains the ranked eligible set, full criterion breakdowns, veto reasons, hard exclusions and remaining identity or access gaps.

Page counts identify a particular edition, format, language, edition year and evidence source. A nominated series needs the complete set; partial volume counts are insufficient. Audio duration and ebook location counts are not pages. Source count measures distinct recorded source IDs, not independent endorsements.

6. Assess domains and connections separately

The model has 20 domains: science; mathematics and uncertainty; computing; high technology; engineering; energy; materials; production; quality and reliability; logistics; trade; scaling; innovation; institutions; economics; finance; politics and state capacity; military capability; strategy; and practical reasoning.

Each domain uses a supported depth from 0 to 4, with rationale, references and confidence. An unassessed domain is unknown, not zero. These values supplement the 15-rule evaluation and start with no scoring weight.

A connection records a specific causal or constraint mechanism involving two or more domains. Each participant needs a supported positive domain value and a distinct role. Record scope, strength, confidence and evidence. Strength progresses from a stated link (1), to developed causal steps (2), supported constraints or feedback (3), and a sustained, bounded explanation (4).

Keep genuine mechanisms involving four, five or eight domains intact. Do not replace them with every possible pair or combine unrelated examples to inflate breadth. Join mechanisms only when evidence establishes a specific handoff or feedback within compatible scopes; a shared domain name is insufficient. Repeated mentions earn no connection bonus.

An optional target specifies desired domains, depths, importance, connection strength and confidence. Coverage and weakest coverage measure depth. Connected strength selects the best supported connected explanation; connected domain count and joint-mechanism count distinguish a chain from a single joint mechanism. Integrated fit is LINK × (0.5 + 0.5 × FLOOR / 100). Partial reviews cannot establish exact final connection scores and remain unknown for those measures.

7. Keep custom exploration separate

The Books view starts with the repository's saved evaluation. Any variable can become an independent filter or score term. Normalization uses 0–100 or −100–100 in the controls, corresponding internally to 0–1 or −1–1. Signed weights, direct linear rates, quadratic curves, piecewise scaling and caps allow positive or negative contributions. Custom totals are not forced into 0–100.

Filters move eligible books between two score-sorted lists. They do not turn a book into a repository exclusion or veto. Custom settings leave saved assessments and verdicts intact. Pareto axes can use variables or score combinations; dominance uses actual book points. Three boundaries are shown: the main frontier in red, a second in blue after removing every title on the first, and a third in dark yellow after removing both earlier layers. Tied titles leave together. Only matching books with known coordinates determine these boundaries. Curves are visual guides.

Vetoed and excluded lists are read-only. Excluded books have no scores. Default book columns show year, pages, Amazon ratings and sources alongside score, title and author, selection, and Details. Additional raw values and scoring contributions are optional. The four controls stay above the results. Book details and calculations work offline; full source inputs and historical analyses open online.
