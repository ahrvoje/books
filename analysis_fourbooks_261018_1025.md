## Scope and method

The four lines supplied by the user are the complete candidate list. No link or comment thread was supplied. External sources were used to resolve identity, check intended audience and structure, examine criticism, and verify editions and reader ratings. They did not expand the title list. Full books were not read cover to cover during this assessment; the report uses the available contents, excerpts, reviews and catalogue evidence.

All four titles and author variants were searched across the three cumulative status files, prior Markdown analyses and structured assessment files. None had a prior assessment or status entry. Never at Rest appeared only as a profile example in AGENTS.md; it is now an actual source nomination. There are no previous scores or source numbers to merge. Historical analyses are preserved. The distinction between technological case studies and single-nation history follows the stated scope test in [source 10](analysis_pastedlists_261018_0118.md).

The sequence was identity → hard exclusions → vetoes → scoring → list update. Score confidence is moderate for three books and low for Morris. Eligibility confidence is high for all four. Confidence in the absence of an established veto is moderate for three and low for Morris. Scores are reading-priority judgments for the specified reader, not measurements; small differences should not decide reading order.

## ASML's Architects: The Story of the Engineers Who Shaped the World's Most Powerful Chip Machines

**René Raaijmakers · 2017 · 89/100 · Recommend.** Score confidence: **moderate**. New assessment, source 43.

Best fit in this set: the conversion of difficult physics and engineering into a viable industrial system. The main chronology reaches 1996; this is an account of ASML's origins, not a full history of its later EUV business.

**Hard exclusions:** Passes both hard exclusions. The author calls this the technical version, but explicitly distinguishes its popular historical treatment from specialist technical depth. Its chronological chapters concern people, machines, firms and commercial survival. Technical explanation supports the history; teaching lithography design or machine operation is not its main purpose. The SPIE catalogue label 'monograph' does not determine the classification. Confidence: **high**.

**Evidence:** [Author's preface and introduction, pp. 15–25](https://techwatchbooks.nl/Files/7/117000/117849/Attachments/Product/q2008v2Ijz361027b7735eJb1650e2A1.pdf) · [Contents and sample chapters](https://www.bruna.nl/images/active/InkijkPDF/cb/9789082707427.pdf) · [Author's English publication announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/asmls-architects-available-how-does-its-content-from-why-raaijmakers) · [CiNii: original year and edition record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BD0251171X) · [Elektor: print format and ISBN](https://www.elektor.nl/products/asmls-architects?_fid=ede21371e&_pos=30&_ss=c). ## Never at Rest: A Biography of Isaac Newton

**Richard S. Westfall · 1980 · 85/100 · Recommend.** Score confidence: **moderate**. New assessment, source 43.

The strongest choice here for the history of scientific reasoning and research conduct. Its main cost is the length: over 900 pages, including substantial personal, religious and institutional detail.

**Hard exclusions:** Passes both hard exclusions on purpose and structure. This is a sustained scientific biography, organized around Newton's life and work, rather than a course, procedure manual or specialist mathematical reference. MAA classifies it as General and distinguishes its biographical aim from a technical presentation. Its inclusion as a profile example and its V3 science-biography exception are not substitutes for this check. Confidence: **high**.

**Evidence:** [Cambridge: description and contents](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/never-at-rest/2BF01FCE690260227B0BA9A617238A23) · [MAA review and paperback metadata; retrieved through search-index text](https://old.maa.org/node/105788) · [Oxford Newton Project bibliography](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/bibliography) · [Cambridge chapter: publication and crisis](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/never-at-rest/publication-and-crisis/AFD6155A399E0428EA487231A780BC9A). The allocations below are this assessment's judgments from that evidence.

| Criterion | Points | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| E — Evidence and inferential integrity | 18/20 | Deep archival scholarship supports the account; identified mathematical distinctions and interpretive reconstruction prevent full marks. |
| M — Mechanistic and causal depth | 15/17 | Shows the development of problems, evidence and concepts, though it is not a modern reconstruction of every derivation. |
| U — Transferable strategic utility | 11/15 | Useful for research habits, priority disputes and scientific institutions; fewer direct lessons for industry or strategic decisions. |
| S — Systems integration | 10/12 | Connects scientific work with intellectual commitments, correspondence, institutions and public duties. |

**Veto review:** V1: MAA identifies a problem in distinguishing Wallis's indivisibles from Newton's infinitesimals. Newton's religious and alchemical beliefs are subjects of history, not supernatural claims endorsed as fact by this assessment. No veto established. Decision confidence: **moderate**.

**Edition and identity:** Use 1980 as the original year, supported by the Oxford Newton Project bibliography and library records. Cambridge Core calls it a 1981 biography and lists 31 January 1981, so the 1980/1981 metadata conflict is recorded rather than silently discarded. The linked complete paperback is ISBN 9780521274357, listed by MAA as 1983 and 928 pages; it is not the later shortened biography.

## X, Y & Z: The Real Story of How Enigma Was Broken

**Dermot Turing · 2018 · 84/100 · Consider.** Score confidence: **moderate**. New assessment, source 43.

A compact case of mathematical capability gaining strategic value through intelligence sharing and international cooperation. The institutional story is stronger than the technical explanation of cryptanalysis.

**Hard exclusions:** Passes both hard exclusions. The publisher and specialist review identify a general-reader history of people and cooperation, not training in codebreaking. Incidental technical explanation does not change that purpose. The author is Dermot, not Dermont, Turing. Confidence: **high**.

## Why the West Rules—for Now: The Patterns of History, and What They Reveal About the Future

**Ian Morris · 2010 · 77/100 · Below target.** Score confidence: **low**. New assessment, source 43.

The widest thematic match, but the weakest recommendation of these four. Its broad comparison can generate useful questions; the numerical index and long-range forecasts do not merit the trust one would place in a validated causal model.

**Hard exclusions:** Passes both hard exclusions. The nominated book is comparative explanatory history for general readers. Its separate technical appendix is supporting evidence, not another nominated book, and does not turn the main narrative into specialist training. Confidence: **high**.

**Evidence:** [Profile: description and publication metadata](https://profilebooks.com/work/why-the-west-rules-for-now/) · [Kenneth Pomeranz, How Big Should Historians Think?, 2011, especially pp. 307–311, 314–315 and 326–327](https://escholarship.org/content/qt7xh7h07z/qt7xh7h07z.pdf) · [Ian Morris's response to Ricardo Duchesne in Reviews in History](https://reviews.history.ac.uk/review/1091/) · [Morris, Social Development, October 2010, sections 3.8, 4 and 12](https://pzacad.pitzer.edu/~lyamane/ianmorris.pdf). **Veto review:** V1 is a close call. Kenneth Pomeranz argues that energy use dominates the preindustrial index, that regional units are problematic, and that measurement does not establish the proposed causal account. He nevertheless finds value in the narrative. Morris's response and methodological appendix openly discuss assumptions, error and ways to challenge the model. My judgment is that this establishes a contested and weakly validated synthesis, but not conclusively that its central geography–technology explanation depends on a material falsehood or invalid inference. The no-veto decision has LOW confidence; disclosure alone would not rescue a demonstrated central failure. V2: no evidence-subordinating allegiance established. V3: comparative mechanisms remain. V4: forecasts are vulnerable, but historical explanation supplies substantial independent value. Retain at a low priority rather than treating its model as settled knowledge. Decision confidence: **low**.

**Edition and identity:** First published in 2010. The verified Profile paperback is dated 4 August 2011, ISBN 9781846682087. The first year and purchase-edition year are kept separate.

## Reading order and limits

Start with **ASML's Architects** for industrial capability, then **Never at Rest** for scientific practice. **X, Y & Z** is shorter and makes a useful study of cooperation under secrecy. **Why the West Rules—for Now** is the lowest-priority work here because the breadth and direct fit exceed the strength of its causal validation. It remains included as an eligible, non-vetoed source book.

For Morris, the methodological appendix is evidence of transparency, not a cure for invalid causation. Sensitivity to score changes also does not by itself validate the selected variables. The V1 decision therefore remains open to revision if stronger evidence establishes that a central conclusion requires an invalid inference. No score was calculated by pretending the disputed claims were correct.

Thematic deductions are zero for all four because none is predominantly about a penalized subject. This does not confer an exemption on biographies or technology books. The purpose and balance of each book, stated above, decide the result. No counterfactual veto table is needed, and EXCLUDED_BOOKS.md and VETOED_BOOKS.md remain unchanged.
