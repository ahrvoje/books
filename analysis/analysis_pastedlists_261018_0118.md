## Decision rules and principal corrections

The ten positive maxima sum to **100**. Apply **one -10 deduction** when a listed theme predominates; do not stack overlapping themes. Thus an affected book cannot score above **90**. Twenty of these 48 books receive that deduction. The pasted 97 for *Nature’s Metropolis*, 96 for *The Cigarette Century*, 95 for *Toms River*, 94 for *Stalin, Vol. I*, and 93/94 for *The Power Broker* cannot be totals under the required deduction. The replacement values come from the criteria, not subtraction from unsupported input totals.

Base verdicts are Recommend at 85–100, Consider at 75–84, Selective at 65–74, and Low priority below 65. “Low fit” and “unwanted topic” qualify personal reading priority without introducing a new exclusion or another numerical deduction. The formula can still give a strong book on an unwanted topic a substantial total: *Nature’s Metropolis* receives 82 for its systems account, but is explicitly marked as an unwanted topic. “Essential” and the pasted letter grades are not retained as unexplained labels.

All 48 works are nonfiction narratives or sustained explanations. Academic publication, quantitative material and course adoption do not alone trigger the second hard exclusion. The intended general readership and historical structure of *Power and Plenty*, *Energy and Civilization* and *The Visible Hand* were checked. *Race for Profit* and *Plantation Goods* also present sustained historical accounts for readers beyond specialist training. Individual eligibility notes and evidence appear below.

No serious V1, V2 or V4 was established on the evidence checked. This is a bounded finding, not certification of every claim. Chandler’s efficiency explanation, Starr’s account of professional authority, Tooze’s economic interpretation and Studwell’s development arguments have substantive limits that reduce the relevant criteria. Those criticisms do not by themselves establish a decisive central failure. Every work has identifiable transferable content, stated below, so V3 does not apply. Political subjects and racial-history subjects are not evidence that the author substitutes allegiance for evidence. No counterfactual veto scores are needed.

The thematic boundary concerns the predominant account, not a location or a keyword. *American Genesis* and *The Visible Hand* organize a national history; *Upstart* is organized around strategic modes across foreign, military and economic policy. The latter’s earlier T=-10 is therefore removed, changing **78 → 88**. *The Anarchy* receives -10 because its main historical narrative concerns conquest and rule in India; its corporate mechanisms still receive points. This scope judgment has moderate confidence. *The Idea Factory* and *The Soul of a New Machine* concern a research institution and an engineering project with transferable, internationally relevant content; US location alone is insufficient.

The requested American-tragedy category produces a deliberate asymmetry: *Challenger* and *Command and Control* receive -10 for their US disaster narratives. *Midnight in Chernobyl* is retained at T=0 because reactor failure and information control, rather than environmental history or disease, organize the account. This is a moderate-confidence subject judgment, not a science exception to the rules. *The Great Bridge* remains penalized for its substantial urban/civic frame.
