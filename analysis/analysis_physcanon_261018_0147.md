## Method and decision boundaries

First resolve identity and editions; then apply fiction and technical-book exclusions; then test V1–V4; finally score every remaining work. Grades and stars in earlier user pastes are not priors for these judgments. A low score or unwanted topic is not an exclusion. No series is expanded into unlisted volumes.

Scores are reading-priority judgments for a theoretical physicist with QFT, cosmology and computation experience. Familiar popular physics loses incremental utility and density, while direct physics fit remains high. Score confidence is **moderate (M)** unless **low (L)** is stated. Eligibility confidence is separate. These are assessments, not measured quantities, and one-point differences should not decide a reading order.

Rule 2 turns on primary purpose, audience and structure. Mathematical difficulty, equations, historical expertise or a course reading-list placement do not by themselves establish exclusion. Conversely, accessible prose, interviews, anecdotes and self-study audiences do not exempt technical training or specialist reference. The relevant publisher contents, excerpts or catalog records are linked per work. This was an evidence-based reassessment, not a claim to have freshly read every book in full.

The strongest boundary cases are:

- **Conversations on Quantum Gravity — excluded.** Its editor states that most interviews address specialists or experts. The collection is primarily a specialist survey, even though some interviews reach a wider audience. [Introduction](https://assets.cambridge.org/97811071/68879/excerpt/9781107168879_excerpt.pdf).
- **The KAM Story — retained, moderate eligibility confidence.** The contents combine history, significance, assumptions and applications for mathematically literate non-specialists. Its main purpose is explanation, not proving or applying the specialist method. Neither the supplied popular tag nor desired-profile status overrides the gate. [Contents and preface](https://www.perlego.com/book/851188/the-kam-story-a-friendly-introduction-to-the-content-history-and-significance-of-classical-kolmogorovarnoldmoser-theory-pdf); [specialist review](https://old.maa.org/press/maa-reviews/the-kam-story).
- **The Road to Reality — retained, moderate eligibility confidence.** Its mathematics supports a broad account and critique of physical theory. Optional exercises are not its main purpose. The specialist review distinguishes the book from a textbook; the large reading cost remains in D3. This decision also considers the publisher's general-reader scope. [Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/129420/the-road-to-reality-by-roger-penrose/); [MAA review, available in search-index text](https://old.maa.org/node/105005).
- **Scientific histories and biographies — individually screened.** Pais, Schweber, Jammer, Enz and Mehra/Milton retain a historical or biographical organizing purpose. Technical reconstruction is not automatically a training manual. Villani's equations document research, rather than teach the reader to reproduce its proof. These are moderate-confidence format decisions where stated below; the V3 biography exception does not waive rule 2.

No serious central factual or inferential failure, evidence-subordinating allegiance, absence of transferable content, or primary dependence on hype was established for the eligible set. This is a bounded evidence judgment. Specific limits reduce E, M, U, R or P: autobiographical selection, disputed causal attribution, quantum-biology speculation, epigenetic overstatement and dated frontier physics. Historical accounts are not presented as current technical references.

## Identity and edition corrections

The initial **The KAM Theorem** by Scott Dumas is the same work as the later **The KAM Story** by H. Scott Dumas, established by the full title and ISBN. Landau's coauthor is **Yuri Borisovich Rumer**, not the supplied expansion Gennadii. The Russian bibliography confirms the 1959 first publication and 1963 reissue. [Landau bibliography](https://ufn.ru/ufn98/ufn98_6/Russian/r986k.pdf); [English edition record](https://books.google.com/books/about/What_Is_Relativity.html?id=24MORh74NFQC).

Ulam's memoir is **1976**, not the 1991 reprint; Weinberg's book is **1992**, not the 1994 paperback; Zee's **Quantum Field Theory, as Simply as Possible** is **2023**, with the supplied 2025 paperback. Villani's first publication is the 2012 French original. The Foundations of Science is the separately named 1913 omnibus and overlaps Science and Hypothesis; the other component titles are not separately nominated. The selected On Growth and Form paperback contains the complete revised 1942 text, not the shorter Bonner abridgment. Per-book records below distinguish first year from purchase edition.
