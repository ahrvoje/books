## Retained vetoes

- **Good To Great** — Jim Collins: Retain V1 at moderate confidence: the documented selection of transition dates, missing risk adjustment and weak out-of-period checks undermine the advertised discovery of portable success causes. Collins's acknowledgment of correlation and the framework's practical interest are considered. Later company failures alone are not the reason.
- **The Communist Manifesto** — Karl Marx; Friedrich Engels: Retain V1/V5 at moderate confidence. V5 rests on specific prescriptions in chapter II for coercive class rule and confiscation from emigrants and rebels, together with chapter IV’s forcible overthrow; the manifesto label and economic reform are not the grounds. V1 concerns the central claim of necessary historical victory derived from selective class analogy. Do not retain V2 merely because the argument belongs to an ideological school. Explicit recruitment to communist political organization and action; V6 supplements the existing vetoes.
- **The Biology of Belief** — Bruce H. Lipton: Retain V1 and lift V5: the publisher’s stated central claim jumps from cellular responses and epigenetics to control of biology by energetic messages from thought. Established regulation of gene expression does not establish this proposed mental control. Spiritual language alone does not establish a separate doctrinal veto.
- **The Power of Now** — Eckhart Tolle: Retain V5: awakening, ego-transcendence and spiritual liberation are taught as authoritative truths and practices. Remove the insufficiently specific additional V1 label; metaphysical claims do not automatically create a separate factual veto.
