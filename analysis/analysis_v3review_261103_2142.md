# Strengthened V3: intellectual-content review

3 November 2026, Europe/Warsaw. **6635 structured records screened; 64 explicit focused decisions; 112 existing V3 records rechecked.** 23 hard exclusions, 10 new vetoes, 7 changed numerical scores and 1 restored recommendation. Current membership: 5854 recommended, 758 vetoed, 3128 excluded.

## Rule and review scope

V3 now requires substantial explanatory or transferable content. A few isolated observations do not automatically pass it. Detailed history, comparative cases, meaningful distinctions and scientific biographies retain their value. A single subject, one framework, low personal topic fit or lack of inspection does not establish failure. Hard exclusions come first.

The catalog-wide pass reassesses **saved evidence**, not thousands of newly read books. It checks purpose, claimed transfer, causal content, prior gate reasoning and confidence. Numerical and wording flags select cases; they never assign a veto. Focused decisions below use the prior assessments plus the identified external evidence. No book is claimed to have received a new full-text reading. 1763 records remain marked for direct review because evidence is thin or purpose is not established. Their existing status is retained rather than inventing certainty.

[Complete screening ledger](../data/v3_review_261103/screening-ledger.json), [explicit decisions and prior evidence](../data/v3_review_261103/changes.json), [verification](../data/v3_review_261103/verification.json). Source memberships and all REL/RD inputs are unchanged. Hard-excluded records receive no new score; removed metadata is archived. Existing analyses and the prior compiled catalog remain unchanged.

## Criteria and evidence

E assesses reliability and inference; S assesses connected layers; U assesses reuse; M assesses explanation of causal steps and constraints; D assesses distinct learning per reading effort. A weak causal claim is not automatically a second density deduction. D changes below use recorded repetition or chapter-specific development, never book length alone. Gate confidence is separate from the confidence of an unchanged score. The revised Cashflow Quadrant density estimate has low confidence: it relies on prior content evidence, while the new author description establishes purpose only. Unreviewed density estimates retain their earlier limits.
