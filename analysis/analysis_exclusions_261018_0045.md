## Rule and decision boundary

The second rule excludes course textbooks, self-study textbooks, technical training, specialist technical monographs and references, and instruction manuals. Eligibility requires narrative or sustained explanatory or argumentative writing for reading outside classroom study and specialist training. Judge the primary purpose, audience and organization together. Stories, dialogues, readable prose, useful mechanisms and high scores do not override an instructional or lookup structure. Conversely, difficulty, equations, scholarship or classroom adoption do not alone make a book a textbook.

The explicit narrative requirement also excludes field guides, dictionaries, reference corpora and daily quotation compilations. A broad scientific explanation or philosophical argument can qualify without being a chronological story. General advice, an academic subject or a narrow topic alone is not sufficient evidence of a manual. The confidence below concerns format and eligibility, not factual quality.
