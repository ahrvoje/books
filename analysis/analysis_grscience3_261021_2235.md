## Retained vetoes

- **The Body Keeps the Score** — Bessel van der Kolk: Retain V1 at moderate confidence: the specialist claim audit identifies unsupported central claims about trauma’s effects on brain function and the special efficacy of body-based treatments. This does not imply that trauma is unreal or that all therapies discussed lack evidence; the veto is tied to the book’s overgeneralized explanatory and treatment claims.
- **Sex at Dawn** — Christopher Ryan; Cacilda Jethá: Retain V1 at moderate confidence: the direct specialist review identifies selective primate comparison and omitted pair-bond and forager evidence central to the ancestral thesis. Alternative sexual norms are not the ground.
- **The Hidden Life of Trees** — Peter Wohlleben: V1, moderate: the book builds its central account of forest society on confident claims about family-like care and directed cooperation. Its own preface presents pain, memory and parent-child living as established knowledge; specialist critiques identify key altruistic mother-tree interpretations as unsupported. This finding distinguishes valid fungal networks from the stronger social-purpose claims and does not assert a unanimous scientific judgment.
