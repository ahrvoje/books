## Method and reading priority

E, S and D use their own quadratic profiles; the other seven criteria remain linear. T11 and T12 are separate thematic deductions. H13 uses the assessed historical endpoint, P14 the original production year, and O15 one verified Amazon rating aggregate. Unknown Amazon counts receive the required zero-deduction fallback, not a claim of popularity. Calculations and sorting retain full precision; displayed values use two decimals.

Verdicts: Recommend ≥85; Consider ≥75; Selective ≥65; Low priority ≥50; Poor fit <50. Confidence describes evidence strength, not statistical probability. Reader aggregates are separate from the quality score. A missing rating is shown as unavailable; no rating is invented for Book Marks, LibraryThing, StoryGraph, Goodreads or Amazon.
