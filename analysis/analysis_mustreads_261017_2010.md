## Method and decision rules

1. Resolve the source title, author, genre and first publication year. Discard fiction before scoring.
2. Apply V1 (serious epistemic failure), V2 (ideological capture), V3 (no transferable intellectual payload, with the specified science/high-tech biography exception), and V4 (hype or ephemerality dependence). A topic preference or political position is not sufficient evidence of a veto.
3. Score every retained title on all ten criteria. Apply T=-10 once when a listed negative theme predominates; otherwise T=0. Several unwanted themes do not stack.
4. Reassess all overlaps using the prior reports and the new source. Preserve all contributing source numbers. Keep low-scoring books in the ranked and master tables.

E = evidence and inference; M = mechanism and causal depth; U = transferable strategic utility; S = systems integration; D = density and earned length; R = durability; F = direct domain fit; A = authority; N = narrative clarity; P = prose precision. **Total = E+M+U+S+D+R+F+A+N+P+T.** Weights are 20,17,15,12,10,9,7,5,3,2, followed by T=0 or -10. All allocations below are earned points, not percentages to be weighted again.

Verdicts are reading-priority labels: **Recommend 85–100; Consider 75–84; Selective 65–74; Low priority 50–64; Poor fit below 50.** These bands do not add an exclusion rule. M and L in the compact table mean moderate and low confidence. Scope, previews, publisher records, reviews and prior evidence support the assessments; this pass is not a cover-to-cover textual audit of all 160 books. No whole-book score is assigned high confidence. Specialist criticism is identified separately from this report's inference.

The scientific biography exception prevents a no-payload veto; it does not automatically cancel T. *American Prometheus*, *The Wright Brothers* and *Midnight in Chernobyl* receive T0 because their main analytical value concerns scientific organization, engineering and system failure with international consequences. *The Great Bridge* receives -10 for its predominant urban-history frame. American authorship alone does not cause a deduction. The systems lessons in disease, racial-history and political books can still earn points. Self-help and sport have low domain fit but are not additional hard exclusions or automatic rule-11 deductions.
