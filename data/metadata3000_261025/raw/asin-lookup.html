    How it works

    Paste an ASIN, any Amazon product URL (the ASIN and the marketplace are read from it), or a UPC / EAN / ISBN. For a code, the lookup first searches the marketplace and takes the first exact product match. It then fetches the product page for the marketplace you picked through a licensed marketplace-data provider and shows what the public page says: title, brand, the price shown to shoppers in that country, the star rating and how many ratings, availability, Amazon's "bought in past month" figure when the page shows one, categories, the product information table, the variation ASINs, and the top reviews.

    Results are cached for seven days so repeat lookups of the same ASIN cost nothing and load instantly; the card says when it came from the cache. Every lookup gets a shareable link that re-opens the result for seven days. Nothing connects to your seller account and nothing is scraped from our servers.

    What the card shows

    Price
    The price on the public product page for the chosen marketplace at lookup time, in that marketplace's currency; a range when variations differ.
    Rating and ratings count
    The star average and the number of ratings the page shows.
    Bought in past month
    Amazon's own rounded sales signal ("2K+ bought in past month"), shown only where the page shows it.
    Availability
    Whether the page offered the product for purchase at lookup time.
    Variations
    The sibling ASINs (sizes, colours) grouped under the same parent; click one to look it up.
    Top reviews
    The reviews Amazon features on the product page, with their star rating and date.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is an ASIN?

    The Amazon Standard Identification Number: a 10-character code (letters and digits, usually starting with B0) that identifies one product on Amazon. Books use their ISBN-10 as the ASIN. It appears in every product URL after /dp/ and in the Product information section of a listing. Every variation of a product (size, colour) has its own ASIN, grouped under a parent ASIN.

    How do I find a product by UPC, EAN, or ISBN?

    Paste the barcode digits into the box. The lookup searches the marketplace for that code and shows the first exact product match, with its ASIN, so you can go from a supplier's UPC or a book's ISBN to the Amazon listing. A code with no match on the chosen marketplace returns "not found"; try another marketplace, since the same product can be listed in one country only.

    Can I look up the ASIN from a UPC, or the UPC from an ASIN?

    UPC to ASIN, yes: enter the UPC and the result card shows the ASIN. ASIN to UPC, only when the listing publishes it in its product information, which is rare; Amazon does not expose the GTIN behind an ASIN as a rule, and the card says so when it is missing.

    Where does the data come from?

    From a licensed marketplace-data provider that reads the public Amazon product page for the marketplace you pick, at the moment of your lookup (results are cached for a week, and the card says when it came from the cache). Nothing here scrapes Amazon from our servers or connects to your seller account. The figures are what the public page shows: the price and availability a shopper in that country sees, the star rating and count, and Amazon's "bought in past month" figure where the page shows one.

    Why does the price differ from what I see on Amazon?

    Amazon shows different prices to different shoppers: by marketplace, by delivery address, by Prime status, by seller (the Buy Box winner changes), and by the variation selected. The lookup reports the price on the public page for the chosen marketplace at lookup time, without a signed-in account. Check the Amazon Price Checker to compare the same ASIN across marketplaces.

    Does this work for every Amazon marketplace?

    Eleven: the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Australia, India, and Mexico. The marketplace is detected from a pasted URL and can be changed in the menu. Japan, Brazil, the UAE, and other sites are not covered.

    Can I export the result?

    Yes. Export CSV downloads the product card as a two-column file (field, value) including the product information rows, ready for a spreadsheet. Each lookup also gets a shareable link that re-opens the same result for seven days without spending another lookup.

    Is the Amazon ASIN lookup free?

    Yes, free with no signup. Lookups draw from a shared daily allowance of Amazon lookups shown by the quota chip, because each uncached lookup costs us a data call; signing in on a paid Keywords Everywhere plan raises the daily cap. Every plan has a cap, higher tiers simply get a higher one.
