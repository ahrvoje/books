He gained widespread acclaim for his books, including "The Black Swan," which explores the impact of rare and unpredictable events on financial markets and broader systems.

An original work that puts into question the norms in scientific methodology and introduces an innovative way of understanding the universe. In the story, the author studies the origins of complex systems and patterns with rather elementary rules by means of simple computer programs, or so-called "cellular automata. According to the book, this class of simple algorithms may in fact be responsible for much of the complexity in nature, from the growth of plants to the human brain. Perilous Interventions

4.4
79 ratings
Hardeep Singh Puri
Recommended by: Nassim Nicholas Taleb
Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Solid Book on Interventionism, Should be Mandatory Reading in Foreign Affairs

source
Consider some of the unintended consequences of international interventions in sovereign nations. This compelling analysis looks into some of the recent conflicts whereby global powers intervened in the cloak of humanitarian assistance or peace enforcement, yet more often than not led to exacerbated instability, violence, and a lot of sufferings among the local population. It critically addresses the grounds, legality, and efficacy of such interventions through detailed case studies, which underline a complicated mesh of geopolitics and great human toll. It calls for a refashioning of world policy and practice into a more responsible and truly supportive way in dealing with world conflict and crisis.
