## In brief

Ross and Tyler argue that companies can make revenue growth predictable by building a systematic process for generating leads, particularly through outbound prospecting. The key is specialisation: splitting sales into distinct roles, so that some people handle inbound leads, some focus exclusively on outbound prospecting, some close deals and some manage existing customers. Ross describes the approach he developed at Salesforce, which he calls Cold Calling 2.0: rather than making cold calls, prospectors send short emails to senior executives asking to be referred to the right person. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
