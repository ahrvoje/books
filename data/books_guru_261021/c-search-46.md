# Are You Ready to Make Your Mark? Introducing the New Book from 99U.

Are You Ready to Make Your Mark? Introducing the New Book from 99U.

Published November 4, 2014 by Jocelyn K. Glei

We believe that the most exciting businesses today aren’t profit-driven, or even product-driven, they’re purpose-driven. And we want to see more of them. That’s why we created the newest installment in our 99U book series, Make Your Mark: The Creative’s Guide to Building a Business with Impact.

U nlike your standard-issue business books, Make Your Mark tackles the topic of building a business from a maker's perspective, rather than a manager's. It's not about manufacturing more widgets or having a great meeting or even getting your startup to that mythical, magical IPO, it’s about how to make stuff that really matters—and build businesses with meaning.

Finally, a business book for makers, not managers.

To get road-tested advice on just what makes a great business, we tapped 21 designers, founders, and entrepreneurs from some of the world’s most creative companies—big and small—to share their wisdom.

## The 99U Book Series:

The world has changed, and the way we work has to change, too. The 99U book series addresses this new 24/7, always-on work landscape and offers pragmatic, actionable advice for managing your time, your career, and your business.

Vol 1: Manage Your Day-to-Day — Make great creative work, every day.

Vol 2: Maximize Your Potential — Cultivate a career on your terms.

Vol 3: Make Your Mark — Build a business that matters.

### More about Jocelyn K. Glei

A writer and the founding editor of 99U, Jocelyn K. Glei is obsessed with how to make great creative work in the Age of Distraction. Her latest book is Unsubscribe: How to Kill Email Anxiety, Avoid Distraction, and Get Real Work Done. Her previous works include the 99U’s own bestselling book series: Manage Your Day-to-Day, Maximize Your Potential, and Make Your Mark. Follow her @jkglei.
