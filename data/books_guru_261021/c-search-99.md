The global eradication of smallpox has been rightly called the “greatest life-saving achievement in the history of medicine,” as pointed out by Richard Preston in the foreword to the new book Smallpox—The Death of a Disease: The Inside Story of Eradicating a Worldwide Killer. This personal account of the eradication effort, written by its director Donald A. Henderson, provides insight into the challenges of this crucial endeavor.
