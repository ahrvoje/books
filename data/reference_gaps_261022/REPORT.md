Counts are the union of inclusive printed-page ranges in the documented reference sections. Indexes, image credits, acknowledgments, and disclosures are excluded. Contents boundaries can include a blank final page. Catalog-only records can omit separately unlisted notes. Counts are edition-specific; they must not be divided by an unrelated edition's total pages.

The search covered title/author web queries, Open Library editions, catalog records, and publisher samples where available. Search results and failed retrievals are retained in web/, library.json, samples.json, and bwb.json. An unsuccessful retrieval does not establish that a book lacks references.
