The Intentional Engineer

What You Will Learn

The Intentional Engineer is full of gems. Here are just a few highlights:

Chapter One: Start Where You Are

No matter the current situation of your life and career, get started on your own path of creating a life of intention.

Chapter Two: Design With Intention

Identify your core values, prototype your way forward, and improve your decision making.

Chapter Three: The Power of Mindsets

Discover how mindsets impact everything you do, and some of the key mindsets you'll want to change to live up to your potential.

Chapter Four: Shift Your Mindset

Go through the step-by-step process to upgrade your mindset in order to make meaningful changes in your life and career.

Chapter Five: Get Career Clarity

Discover what you truly care about so that you can focus and accelerate your progress. Get help and insights from others along the way!

Chapter Six: Discover Your Genius Zones

Your genius zones are a key to your satisfaction and success. Get in flow, identify your genius zones, and learn how to leverage them in a big way!

Chapter Seven: Control Your Narrative by Growing Your Personal Brand

You have a personal brand whether you create it or not. Utilize yours in all of your critical interactions!

Chapter Eight: Skills that Support Your Intentions

Learn the transformative power of communication, accountability, feedback, and relationships to support your intentional life.
