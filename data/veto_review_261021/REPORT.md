## Method and limits

Each book has eight gate findings, evidence, confidence and a complete score review. V4 is not a ban on forecasting; V5 is not a ban on advocacy or criticism. Another veto requires its own serious, evidenced defect. The prior analyses are read as evidence and history, not automatic authority. The non-book exclusion inserted during this review is recorded in rules-amendment.json without changing the baseline; all three hard exclusions are applied. Stable gate names avoid the resulting numbering shift. The old references to both and either in AGENTS remain a wording concern for later review. Scores are retained when the gate interpretation changes without new evidence about quality. All fifteen inputs are reviewed; a restored book can still be a poor fit.

Primary pages, samples and retained analysis are used where available. This is not a claim to have reread every complete book or recaptured all source comments. Unknown Amazon counts retain their recorded fallback; no new ratings observation is claimed. Restored page/domain records are existing evidence, not new research. 34 missing-domain indexes are derived from their existing supported domain keys.
