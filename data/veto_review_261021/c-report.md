# Packet C: review of 117 current vetoes

Review date: 2026-10-21, Europe/Warsaw. Confidence varies by book and is recorded separately for eligibility and score.

The final decisions are in `c-decisions.json`. All 117 input IDs occur once: **63 recommended, 53 vetoed, 1 hard-excluded**. The retained vetoes comprise **22 V1, 2 V2, 16 V3, 4 V4 and 9 V5**. Every record has the stable named gate findings `BOOK_FORM`, `FICTION`, `REFERENCE`, `V1`–`V5`. All nominated items are published books or a published book series; none fails the book-form rule. The inserted non-book rule and renumbering do not change these named identities.

Each decision was made individually from the prior book-level assessments and supporting material. `c-findings.txt` records those individual judgments; `c-build.mjs` serializes them and checks coverage and structure. `c-notes.txt` replaces repeated or grouped explanations with criterion-specific reasons, preserving numerical attainments. It does not select verdicts by words in titles, author identity or political affiliation. Prior analyses remain unchanged. The new decisions do not add titles or alter source membership.

## Main changes

- **The Singularity Is Near — release, moderate eligibility confidence.** Examination of the actual book, not only its contents list, found discussion of limits, counterarguments, regulation and imperfect defenses. Its dates remain uncertain, but the earlier forecast-only reading missed substantial constraints and reusable ideas. See the [book text, chapters 8–9](https://mriquestions.com/uploads/3/4/5/7/34572113/singularityisnear.pdf), the author's [explanation of the forecast](https://www.thekurzweillibrary.com/singularity-q-a) and [response to a critic](https://www.thekurzweillibrary.com/kurzweil-responds-dont-underestimate-the-singularity).
- **This Is for Everyone — release, high eligibility confidence.** The inventor memoir has independent technical and institutional value. A proposal for user control and an open web does not satisfy current V5. See the [author's description](https://thisisforeveryone.timbl.com/about-the-book.html).
- **The Ones We've Been Waiting For — release, high eligibility confidence.** Its introduction limits individual predictions and does not claim the younger generation will save the country. The earlier veto overstated the forecast. See the [publisher's actual introduction](https://penguinrandomhousehighereducation.com/book/?isbn=9780525561521).
- **The Third Wave, The Truth Machine, Virtual Society and Why Buy Bitcoin — release.** Each has mechanisms, institutional constraints or risk discussion independent of a promised future. The releases do not validate adoption dates or price targets. Primary evidence is linked in each decision.
- **The Fourth Turning — release, moderate confidence.** The [actual text, PDF pages 167–170 and 375–377](https://www.rexresearch1.com/Books/StraussHowe4Turning.pdf) acknowledges uncertain outcomes, irregular cycles and alternative scenarios. Its low evidence and causal scores remain; absence of validated forecasting alone no longer supports V4.
- **The Innovation Delusion, The Federalist Papers, The Law, The New Jim Crow, The Road to Wigan Pier, Whole Earth Discipline, Zucked and other political programs — release.** Advocacy, reform, activism or a manifesto label is no longer a sufficient V5 basis. Their remaining quality limitations stay in the individual scores.
- **The Soul of the World, The Spiritual Brain, Things Hidden Since the Foundation of the World, The Myth of Christian Beginnings, Think on These Things, Total Freedom and The Tao of Wu — release.** Sacred experience, a nonmaterialist hypothesis, interpretation of religion and spiritual memoir do not by themselves establish authoritative religious doctrine. The exact boundaries and confidence are stated per work. Scruton's [own explanation](https://www.rogerscruton.com/articles/255-soul-of-the-world) expressly distinguishes his argument from proving God or religious truth. The [Spiritual Brain introduction](https://paperzz.com/doc/8425244/spiritual-brain---thedivineconspiracy.org) expressly denies that the nun-imaging study proves God.
- **The Ethics of Money Production and Thou Shall Prosper — release, moderate confidence.** Economic or business arguments using religious and cultural premises are not automatically doctrinal works. Hülsmann's [actual introduction, note 25 on printed page 15](https://cdn.mises.org/The%20Ethics%20of%20Money%20Production_2.pdf) rejects a separate Christian economics. Lapin's [publisher description and contents](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/finance-economics-law/finance-investments-13fi/personal-finance-13fi2/thou-shall-prosper-978-0-470-48588-0) identify business principles as the subject. Their evidence scores remain low.
- **The Gallic Wars — release, moderate confidence.** Participant self-justification is not itself V2. The text records Roman losses and wrongdoing, which defeats a blanket claim that all adverse evidence is suppressed. This leaves substantial reliability limits in the score; it does not endorse conquest. See [Book VII](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.02.0001%3Abook%3D7).
- **The Hockey Stick Illusion — release, moderate confidence.** Its subject includes replication, data access and statistical reconstruction, which can outlast the immediate dispute. The previous record did not substantiate a replacement serious V1 or V2. Low evidence and precision scores remain; release does not certify its climate conclusions. See the [author's account of the book](https://bishophill.squarespace.com/blog/2009/11/21/the-hockey-stick-illusion.html%3Bjsessionid%3DB268527F7E2C7599B2BA07DC82F9A80F.v5-web013).
- **Thermoinfocomplexity — release, low confidence.** A broad unvalidated proposal and limited accessible evidence do not establish serious dependence on hype. No claim is made that the theory has been validated. Its quality marks remain low.
- **Timeline of World History — hard-exclude under REFERENCE, high confidence.** The publisher describes fold-out charts and timelines in an 80-page visual reference, not a sustained narrative or explanatory account. No new score or counterfactual is assigned. See the [publisher's description](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Timeline-of-World-History/Matt-Baker/9781645174172).

## Retained boundaries

The four retained V4 decisions are **The End of the World Is Just the Beginning, The Future Is Faster Than You Think, The Real Crash and The Rise of Superman**, each at moderate confidence. They concern categorical collapse or premature transformation/performance claims. They are not vetoed merely for making predictions. Zeihan's [actual book extract](https://zeihan.com/credit-and-the-end-of-the-world/) treats uncertainty as principally timing; Schiff's [opening chapter](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781427222947/therealcrash/) makes the crisis inevitable. The published [flying-car extract](https://members.real-leaders.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/How-to-build-your-future.pdf) declares the promised transition already present from demonstrations and company targets.

The nine retained V5 works have a central scriptural, doctrinal or authoritative spiritual-teaching identity: **The Dude De Ching, The Four Agreements, The Great Challenge, The Heart of the Buddha's Teaching, The Holy Bible, The Mystery of the Grail, The Power of Now, The Zen Teaching of Huang Po and Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind**. The decisions do not use political affiliation or a manifesto label as a substitute for current V5 evidence.

The retained V1/V2 decisions use the independently recorded book-specific defects. Confidence is moderate where the case rests on a contested specialist interpretation or source with a relevant affiliation. For example, the CIA affiliation of the critic of *The Devil's Chessboard* remains explicit. The retained V3 cases are individual findings about developed intellectual transfer; neither a memoir label nor an unwanted topic is sufficient by itself. Science and high-tech memoir exceptions were considered.

Additional unsupported gates were removed from retained works: V5 from *The Law That Never Was*, *The Matter with Things*, *The New Left / The Return of the Primitive*, *The Real Crash* and *Warrior Pose*; a separately insufficient V1 from *The Mystery of the Grail* and *The Power of Now*.

## Limits and verification

This was a complete decision review of the packet, not a new cover-to-cover reading of all 117 works. Previous book-level analyses and captured evidence were reassessed; primary text or publisher/author material was sought where it could decide a changed boundary. An unavailable chapter was not treated as proof that the chapter lacks safeguards. A marketing promise alone was not treated as an entire book's evidence.

The full *Singularity* and *Spiritual Brain* passages were inspected through reproductions of the primary text; their hosting sites are not the publishers. The 2015 paperback and 2017 ebook descriptions of *Thermoinfocomplexity* do not by themselves prove a new work distinct from the recorded 2013 lineage, so the original-year input is retained and the identity/edition lineage remains a review concern. No new aggregate rating was inferred from those edition records.

Validation checks all 117 IDs, eight named gates per record, ten criterion notes for all 116 scored works, no score for the hard exclusion, existing local evidence paths, status/veto consistency, and absence of obsolete active-veto wording in released criterion notes. The six numeric changes were independently recalculated from raw attainment and deductions. No canonical ledger or site file was edited by this packet.
