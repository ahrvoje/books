the job—ordinary photons of visible light would suffice. Indeed, it is easier

side was moving on.

## Quantum Books to read

In Computing with Quantum Cats: From Colossus to Qubits John Gribbin argues for a universe of many parallel worlds where ‘everything is real’. Looking back to Alan Turing’s work on the Enigma machine and the first electronic computer, Gribbin explains how quantum theory developed to make quantum computers work in practice as well as in principle.
