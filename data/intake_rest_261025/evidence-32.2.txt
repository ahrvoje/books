## Shariah Values. In Part I “Value Systems Behind Institutions” (3–25)

Professor Çizakça describes the two contrasting approaches to Islamic economics
and finance:
• the dominant “Shariah compliant approach” based on conventional banking
borrowed from the West which now prevails; and
• a potential truly “Shariah based” value system drawn from a profound
historical understanding of Islamic financial institutions and how they
operated over centuries.
