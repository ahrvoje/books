L123@P3: Unfortunately, one has to begin with some irritating side
L124@P3: issues. As these deal with Kuran’s personal attitudes, most reviewers

L126@P3: Bias does not destroy or even negate scholarship, provided the bias is
L127@P3: recognized. Why would one be interested in a subject if one did not
L128@P3: have some attraction or revulsion toward it in the first place? Even if
L129@P3: one began one’s research out of curiosity, how often does this continue
L130@P3: unless we find a moral interest in continuing with the study? One
L131@P3: would hope that a book about religious law, claiming to be an

L135@P3: Consider Kuran’s presentation of the Hajj (the primary
L136@P3: pilgrimage of Islam) as “the backdrop for a trade fair” (p. 46). Later, he

L139@P3: Abraham to God. It is because the spot was holy that pilgrims traveled
L140@P3: to Mecca. The crowds that arrived attracted traders. Eventually, the

L169@P4: 3. Kuran’s Argument Summarized
L170@P4: Kuran is clear about his message: “The Middle East fell behind
L171@P4: the West because it was late in adopting key institutions of the modern
L172@P4: economy” (p. 5). The institutions that Kuran considers critical are the
L173@P4: formation of large capitalist associations, such as joint-stock
L174@P4: companies, the recognition of companies as legal entities, and the
L175@P4: concomitant introduction of impersonal exchange. Muslim law
L176@P4: impeded the formation of such essential features of a modern economy
L177@P4: by its inability to consider the corporation as a legal person. These
L178@P4: large concentrations were further inhibited by Muslim inheritance
L179@P4: laws, which spread the wealth of the deceased. When concentrations
L180@P4: did occur, as in the waqf (trust), these were inimical to progress since
L181@P4: the founders’ wishes bound all future generations. Muslim law was the
L182@P4: backbone of each of the above problems, so it is Muslim law that one
L183@P4: has to point to when assigning blame. In what follows, I try to follow
L184@P4: Kuran’s order as far as possible, but have occasionally altered the
L185@P4: sequence for clarity.

L190@P4: variations across societies” (p. 10). Furthermore, the explanation is
L191@P4: inadequate because it ignores the facts that Islamic law was adaptive
