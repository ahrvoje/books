British Propeller Makers of World War I by Lt. Col. Bob Gardner. Section NAL Hub  

"There has been a Lang BE2 propeller in my family for as long as I can remember. My father was apprenticed at Waring and Gillows before the war and was one of those experienced wood workers from as many as 125 companies who turned their hands from pullman coach and ocean liner interiors to propeller making. Little did know when I was looking to find out the meaning of the many mysterious code letters stamped on the hubs that a five-volume work on the shelves at the NAL would make it all clear. A number of blade design drawings are also included and the photos, mainly in colour of factories, workshops, company logos and propellers from 8 to 20 feet in diameter bring the books alive."
