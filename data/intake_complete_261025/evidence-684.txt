## Abstract

Socrates has a unique position in the history of philosophy. Had it not been for his influence on Plato, the whole development of Western philosophy might have been unimaginably different since Socrates wrote nothing himself, and our knowledge of him is derived primarily from the engaging and infuriating figure who appears in Plato's dialogues. Socrates: A Very Short Introction explores the relationship between the historical Socrates and the Platonic character, and examines the enduring image of Socrates as the ideal exemplar of the philosophic life — a thinker whose moral and intellectual integrity permeated every detail of his life, even in the face of betrayal and execution by his fellow Athenians.

Keywords: Aeschines, Alcibiades, Antisthenes, Aristotle, Diogenes Laertius, life, Plato, Protagoras, Socrates, Xenophon
