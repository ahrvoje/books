Metal-working machine shops, used for cold shaping of iron, depended on foundry castings, rods and sheet metal. The Kendall & Lawrence Gun Shops were known in nineteenthcentury terminology as “the works,” a complex with several forges, a wood-working shop, a machine shop, a drafting room, a pattern room and a building to assemble finished articles. A famous 1849 illustration of “the works” and the Armory features at least seven one-and-a-half- and two-story buildings (with a total of sixteen chimneys), while an 1853 illustration features at least nine buildings on the north bank of Mill Brook.²¹
