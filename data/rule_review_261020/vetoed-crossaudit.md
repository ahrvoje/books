# Independent H2 boundary audit, 2026-10-20

Scope: screen the 270 H2-retained entries in agent A's draft, then inspect the old reasons and primary evidence for the strongest mathematical and practitioner-narrative boundary cases. This is a second boundary check, not a fresh full-text assessment of all 270 books. No canonical ledger, score, source membership, or agent A decision file was edited here. Findings were sent directly to agent A and the integrating agent.

The revised rule expressly allows expert-for-expert books with at least some narrative structure when their purpose is not examination, instructions, or reference. A technical audience, equations, exercises, or possible classroom use alone does not establish that disqualifying purpose. Conversely, a few anecdotes do not change a procedure manual into an explanatory narrative.

Its general-reader audience makes the phrase “specialist reference” inadequate by itself; the final decision must instead explain whether the book lacks the required sustained account. This audit did not obtain a sufficient primary sample to settle that structural question.

## Good to Great and Built to Last: separate V1 check

H2 eligibility does not settle V1. **Good to Great** has stronger adverse evidence than the observation that some featured firms later declined. Resnick and Smunt's published analysis challenges the choice of transition dates, missing risk adjustment and significance tests, and the lack of later-period validation. Their university's report also records that the framework aided planning. These are separate claims: practical usefulness can remain while the advertised causal inference fails. [Wake Forest report of the study](https://business.wfu.edu/newsroom/babcock-professors-scrutinize-methodology-in-best-selling-business-book/).

The balancing evidence is Collins's use of matched comparison firms and his long observed performance windows. His own account also makes strong portable claims that the selected practices explain how other firms can become great. Thus the central causal claim needs explicit V1 adjudication; a generic low evidence score does not by itself answer whether the flaw is serious enough for the gate. [Collins's own 2001 account](https://www.jimcollins.com/article_topics/articles/good-to-great.html).

**Built to Last** must receive a separate finding. The Good to Great study cannot be copied onto a different sample and research design. Rosenzweig explains the general problem of inferring leadership and culture from known performance, but that argument must be connected to this book's actual data before applying V1. [Rosenzweig's account of the halo problem](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/the-halo-effect-and-other-managerial-delusions).

These competing points and the primary links were sent to agent A for its pending final V1 decision. No new counterfactual score was assigned by this audit.
