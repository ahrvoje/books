# Boundary checks for the revised rules

Review date: 2026-10-20, Europe/Warsaw. These checks supplement the individual decisions; they do not nominate new titles.

- **The Princeton Companion to Mathematics:** the [editor's preface](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/releases/gowers/gowers_preface.pdf), p. x, explicitly identifies reference use, with lookup articles and cross-references. Historical essays and extended explanatory surveys are real, but the revised exception also requires that expert works not be explicitly intended as reference. Retain H2, moderate confidence. An earlier draft of this review gave insufficient weight to that final condition; no new score is published.
- **The Princeton Companion to Applied Mathematics:** the [preface](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/p10592.pdf), p. ix, identifies an organized reference work. Its selective coverage, history and explanatory essays distinguish it from an exhaustive handbook, but do not remove the explicit reference purpose. Retain H2, moderate confidence, under the same final condition of the expert-reader exception.
- **Build, Tony Fadell:** the [publisher's English description on its translated-edition page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/729967/crea-una-guia-poco-ortodoxa-para-hacer-cosas-que-marquen-la-diferencia--build-an-unorthodox-guide-to-making-things-worth-making-by-tony-fadell/) describes cumulative entries that follow a career through product design, leadership and company formation. That narrative and general argument support admission despite its advice-reference format. Confidence: moderate. This page establishes structure, not the original English publication date.
- **A Pattern Language:** the [authors' introduction](https://www.patternlanguage.com/labyrinth/apl-tour2.html) and [book description](https://www.patternlanguage.com/bookstore/bookshelf.html) identify a system of practical design solutions extending to detailed physical construction. The primary purpose remains guidance for carrying out design and building work. Retaining H2 has moderate confidence. This judgment does not apply automatically to Alexander's separate theoretical works.

The decisive distinction is purpose and structure under the revised exception. Labels such as “encyclopedia,” “technical,” “course” or “advice” do not decide eligibility by themselves.

- **Karl Marx's Theory of History, G. A. Cohen:** the [publisher's chapter extracts](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv105b973) distinguish interpreting Marx from evaluating truth, and the expanded edition revises the author's confidence in historical materialism. This sustained theoretical argument does not establish explicit ideological campaigning. Admission has moderate eligibility confidence; contested functional explanation reduces the score.
- **Man, Economy, and State, Murray Rothbard:** the [author's preface](https://mises.org/online-book/man-economy-and-state-power-and-market/preface-revised-edition-muray-n-rothbard) supports treatise status and openly states the deductive method. That method and universal claims need critical assessment, but Austrian-school affiliation alone is not V5. The original 1962 work is distinct from the later *Power and Market*. Admission remains a moderate-confidence gate judgment with reduced evidence and causal-depth attainment.
- **Good to Great:** the [research authors' account](https://business.wfu.edu/newsroom/babcock-professors-scrutinize-methodology-in-best-selling-business-book/) identifies data-dependent transition dates and missing risk/significance adjustments in the company's success classification. These bear directly on the claimed portable success factors. Retain V1 at moderate confidence, while recording the framework's practical value and Collins's qualification about causal inference. Later company failures alone do not establish the veto. This finding does not automatically apply to *Built to Last*.
