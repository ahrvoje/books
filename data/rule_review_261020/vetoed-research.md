The accompanying excerpt file records the relevant prior book sections recovered from 62 historical analysis files; every input has at least one section. This is not a claim that every cited external page was newly retrieved or that every book was read in full.

The changed rule permits an explanatory book to have policy conclusions, civilizational preferences, moral proposals or visions. Those features do not by themselves establish an explicit ideological text, manifesto or declaration of activism. A narrative about an activist's life is not automatically a declaration of activism. An author's affiliations do not substitute for the book's purpose. Serious independent V1–V4 grounds remain in force. Base or derived religious teaching remains V5, but merely discussing religion or describing religious experience does not settle that classification.
