RP Vegan by Lori Shaw is a plant-based eCookbook from Renaissance Periodization, featuring 50 high-protein, macro-friendly vegan recipes. Designed to align with RP’s nutrition templates, this cookbook offers practical meal options for those following a vegan diet while aiming to meet specific macronutrient goals. Here are the Key features:

Image: rp vegan RP book Lori Shaw

  * 50 High-Protein Vegan Recipes: Each recipe is crafted to provide substantial protein content, supporting muscle maintenance and growth within a vegan framework.
  * Macro-Friendly Design: Detailed macronutrient information accompanies each recipe, allowing for precise tracking and adjustments to fit individual dietary needs.
