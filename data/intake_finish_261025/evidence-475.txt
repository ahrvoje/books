## About Ali Abdaal

Ali Abdaal is a doctor, entrepreneur, amateur magician, and the world’s most-followed productivity expert. Ali first became interested in psychology while juggling the demands of medical training at Cambridge University with building his business. While working as a doctor in the UK's National Health Service, Ali started to document his journey towards living a healthier, happier, more productive life on his YouTube channel. Today, Ali has 8 million followers across the internet, and is one of the world’s most-trusted voices on psychology. His work has been featured in the Financial Times, Forbes, the BBC, Business Insider and Good Morning America. Ali’s first book, Feel-Good Productivity, was an instant New York Times and Sunday Times bestseller; it has been published in more than 30 languages and sold over 400,000 copies worldwide.
