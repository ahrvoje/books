Harvard method of listing references at the end of the text

References should be listed in alphabetical order by author’s name and then by date (earliest first).  If more than one item has been published in a particular year, they should be distinguished by the use of supplementary letters (2010a, 2010b etc). Whenever possible, details should be taken from the title page of a publication and not from the front cover, which may be different. For books, place of publication as well as publisher name should be stated, separated by a colon..

Each reference should include the elements and punctuation given in the examples on the right. Authors’ forenames should be included in full if given on the title page. The title of the publication should be given in italics if it is a book, but in roman type if it is an article or a chapter in a book..
