strategic, or cultural choice. The computer disperses, becomes ubiquitous,

action where computing, sorting, and interaction occur. The analysis thus

also reflects a context that was shaped by the development dynamics of

computer–aided networks and distributed computing.

“Taking God Out of Mathematics and Putting the Body Back

In”53 was Brian Rotman’s goal in a 1993 essay on the ghost in Turing’s

reference to the work of Alan Turing. For the history of mathematics,

this was thrilling and changed a lot. In contrast, the history of computers
