### Synopsis

An Officer, Not a Gentleman is the remarkable true story of Mandy Hickson, one of the first female fighter pilots to fly the RAF Tornado GR4 on the front line.

Told with honesty, warmth, and humour, Mandy takes readers inside the world of military aviation, from combat missions over Iraq to the teamwork and trust that keeps pilots alive in the skies. But beyond the adrenaline, this is a story about resilience, leadership, and breaking barriers - proving that courage isn’t the absence of fear, but the determination to rise above it.

When she was told she’d have to work hard to be taken seriously, Mandy refused to let convention define her. Instead, she learned how to lead under pressure, make split-second decisions, and inspire others to do the same — lessons that apply as powerfully in business and life as they do in the cockpit.

A powerful memoir of courage, humour, and determination. This is for anyone who believes in pushing boundaries, breaking stereotypes, and learning to fly, whatever their path in life.

# Mandy Hickson’s Post

Mandy Hickson

7mo

Today is a special day. We’re officially releasing our new book: 📚 An Officer and Her Gentleman 📗 This one is particularly meaningful because I’ve written it alongside my husband, Craig Hickson. Between us, we’ve spent decades in the cockpit: fast jets, helicopters, commercial aircraft, RAF and Royal Navy, triumphs and setbacks. But this book isn’t just about aviation. It’s about: • The fear of failure and how to keep going anyway • Human vulnerabilites • Identity, purpose, and starting again • The messy, real, human side of “success” Bringing our stories together has been humbling, emotional, and at times challenging. But it’s also been one of the most rewarding things we’ve done. If you’d like to read it, you can find it in comments. If it resonates, we’d be grateful for a review. Every review genuinely helps the book reach more people. And if you do pick it up, we’d love to hear what stands out for you. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
