Making Modern Science, 2nd Edition by Peter J. Bowler and Iwan Rhys Morus provides a historical survey of the development of science, addressing key episodes and themes from the Scientific Revolution to contemporary issues. The book aims to serve as a textbook for undergraduates while remaining accessible to general readers, incorporating updated research and new chapters in its second edition. It explores the complex relationship between science, society, and history, challenging conventional narratives and emphasizing the multifaceted nature of scientific progress.
