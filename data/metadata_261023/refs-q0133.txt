#### W HAT IS CIVILIZATION?

In an important essay entitled "The History of Civilizations: The Past Explains the Present," Braudel (1980) invited his readers to a meeting of great minds, like Jacob Burckhardt, François Guizot, Oswald Spengler, Arnold Toynbee, and Philip Bagby. For Braudel, the terms civilization and culture are closely linked and sometimes interchangeable. He wrote, "A civilization, we say, is a collection of cultural characteristics and phenomena" (Braudel 1980, p. 77), and he continued,

A civilization is first of all a space, a "cultural area," as the anthropologists would say, a locus. This book was originally written as a high school (final year) textbook of history of civilizations, collected in the series Le Monde actuel, histoire et civilisations, edited by S. Baille, F.
