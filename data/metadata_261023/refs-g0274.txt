GM and Automotive Diesels during the 1920s GM’s involvement in the diesel engine industry grew directly from its production of automobiles. Even in the early years of the twentieth century, diesel-engine advocates realized that the largest potential market for diesels lay in the motor vehicle industry. In 1921, GM engineer Carl E.
