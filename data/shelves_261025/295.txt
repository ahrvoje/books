David Friedberg
David Friedbergbook recommendations
Share
David Friedberg is founder and CEO of The Production Board (TPB), a San Francisco-based investment foundry focused on improving technologies in agriculture, food, and life sciences. Prior to TPB, David was founder and CEO of The Climate Corporation (acquired by Monsanto in 2013).
