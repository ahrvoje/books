Welcome to my virtual bookshelf; a collection of all the books that I have read or am reading. I’ve categorised my reading into six buckets of practical worldly wisdom: numeracy, human nature, history, natural science, business and second-level thinking and ordered my books within them based on their influence upon me.

CURRENTLY READING: Goldsmith M. (2008) What Got You Here Won’t Get You There: How successful people become even more successful

PREVIOUSLY READ: March J. G. (2010) The Ambiguities Of Experience
