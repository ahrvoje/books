L257: ## History
L258: ### General
L259: 
L260:   * War in Human Civilization by Azar Gat (2006)
L261: 
L262: best book on war in existence
L263: 
L264:   * Pre-Industrial Societies: Anatomy of the Pre-Modern World by Patricia Crone (1989)
L265: 
L266: highly recommended for an overview of how, broadly speaking, pre-industrial societies actually operated
L267: 
L268:   * Escape from Rome: The Failure of Empire and the Road to Prosperity by Walter Scheidel (2019)
L269: about why the Roman empire formed, why no later power managed to recreate the empire, and how this is important for understanding why Europe industrialized first
L270: 
L271:   * Why the West Rules—For Now by Ian Morris (2010)
L272: 
L273: long, but serves as a good introduction to both Western and Chinese history, in parallel.
L274: 
L275:   * Foragers, Farmers, and Fossil Fuels, also by Ian Morris (2015)
L276: 
L277: on how the values we hold have changed over time
L278: 
L279:   * The Big Book of Horrible Things by Matthew White (2011)
L280: a fascinating (if somewhat arbitrary) discussion of the 100 worst atrocities in history, ranked by number of deaths. The author also has an (appropriately dark) sense of humor.
L281: 
L282:   * After Tamerlane by John Darwin (2007)
L283: 
L284: a history of European colonialism and the evolution of the great power system over the past five hundred years
L285: 
L286:   * Plagues Upon the Earth by Kyle Harper (2021)
L287: 
L288: a history of diseases and pandemics
L289: 
L290:   * Famine, a short history by Cormac Ó Gráda (2009)
