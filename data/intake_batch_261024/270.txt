L3: # Bookshelf
L4: 
L5: This is a record of books I've read and kept around.
L6: 
L7: For years, schools tricked us into viewing reading like mandatory chores (aka homework). That changed when I started choosing books based on my personal curiosity at any given time, not obligation enforced by someone else.
L8: 
L9: Most books here are physical copies from my bookshelf at home, and the list keeps growing as my interests expand over time.
L10: 
L11: To help you navigate, I've color-coded my recommendations:
L12:   * Orange: Personal favorites that drastically changed how I see the world. These books are either unusually dense with insights or contain a single, transformative idea that provides a new model for the world. These are the rare books whose lessons have stuck with me long after reading.
L13: 
L14:   * Green: Strong recommendations that are well worth reading (and re-reading).
L15: 
L16:   * Rest: Everything else I've found interesting.
L17: 
L18: The book titles are links that take you to Amazon results for that specific book.
L19: #### I hope you find something below that sparks your interest too!
