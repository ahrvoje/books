## Decision boundary

The added rule reads: “Religious books, ideological books, manifests, political of environmental or civil activism.” This review reads “manifests” as manifestos and the final phrase as political, environmental or civil activism. The existing requirement that a veto be present **at a serious level** still applies.

V5 applies to religious scriptures and substantial spiritual teaching; sustained advocacy of an ideological worldview or political program; manifestos; and works with a substantial campaigning purpose. It applies across viewpoints, including liberal, socialist, anarchist, religious, atheist and ecological advocacy. Historical importance, sound arguments, reader ratings and a high score do not override it. V5 does **not** by itself establish factual error under V1 or evidence-distorting allegiance under V2.

A history of religion is not automatically religious teaching. Analysis of economic policy, political institutions, race, a social movement or the environment is not automatically activism. A reform implication, an author's identity or affiliation, or promotional use of the word “important” is insufficient. For mixed works, the governing argument, substantial purpose and structure decide. Borderline retained titles and the confidence in their V5 assessment are listed below. These are classification judgments, not measured properties.

Hard exclusions come first. Fiction such as Atlas Shrugged, The Great Divorce, The Screwtape Letters, Siddhartha and The Prophet stays in EXCLUDED_BOOKS.md; spiritual practice manuals such as The Attention Revolution, The Mind Illuminated and Turning the Mind into an Ally also remain there. They receive no new score or counterfactual score. The two newly identified practical guides below follow the same precedence.

Abolish Rent uses political argument and movement reporting; it likewise receives V5.
